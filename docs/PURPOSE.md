# Purpose

## Built to serve. Made available to everyone.

RIDA began as a paid service. For its first two years, users accessed it through service plans. Around 1,200 people approached the team. Under Qasra Foundation and its CEO's direction, the library was then made available without a RIDA subscription fee for public benefit.

The aim is practical: help developers deliver the part of a collection their application actually needs, using their own storage and hosting. That can benefit small teams, projects with limited budgets and users on limited mobile data.

## Former service plans

These are historical plans described on the project's Purpose page. They are no longer offered for purchase.

| Former plan | Monthly price | Storage | Monthly delivery |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | 500 MB | 10 GB |
| Starter | $15 | 5 GB | 100 GB |
| Pro | $49 | 50 GB | 1 TB |
| Enterprise | Custom quote | Custom | Custom |

Using RIDA now avoids a RIDA service-plan subscription. It does not remove your chosen provider's storage, transfer or request costs. Infrastructure cost estimates are not the same as profit; operating a service can also require support, engineering, payment processing and other costs. The website's [interactive calculator](https://rida.qasra.org/purpose#savings-calculator) lets readers explore its reference hosting scenarios.

## What is available

Install the Python or Node library, build datasets locally, keep your index/map with the application, and serve values from a compatible host. The Mac app provides a graphical local workflow. You choose identifiers, field structure, encryption and hosting.
